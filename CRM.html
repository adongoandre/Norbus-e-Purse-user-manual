CRM Module Overview

Welcome to Norbus e-Purse CRM

A one-stop Customer Relationship Management module for the entire system. The Client Relationship Management (CRM) system is a comprehensive platform designed to manage customer relationships, account setups, and financial services within the Norbus e-Purse ecosystem. This system provides tools for customer onboarding, account management, and compliance monitoring.

The CRM module handles all customer-related operations including:

  • Onboarding New Clients
  • Search functionalities
  • Checking of investment balances
  • Viewing of Statements
  • Maintenance of existing client's information
  • Uploading of KYC documents
  • Customer collateral setups
  • Set Post-No-Debit (PND) on customers
  • Tagging of deceased clients
  • Setting up of Leads

1 Login Credentials: Access the CRM system through the main Norbus e-Purse platform with appropriate user credentials.

Master Setups

Configuration Management

The CRM has a master setup that is used to perform setups of relevant information required for the main customer setup and maintenance pages. These master setups show as drop-downs for selection in the client setup / maintenance pages.

This is used to setup/add RMs under which clients will be assigned. Details of existing RMs could also be edited or deleted.

Clicking on Add after selecting Relationship Managers allows you to add a new RM.

To setup all titles that will be needed during client creation. Eg. Mr. Mrs.

Click on Add after selecting Titles, fill in the required information, submit to checker and save.

For adding new client categories, editing and deleting existing ones. This is tied to the Regulatory Bodies' setup. Every client falls under a client category which in turn falls under a regulatory body.

Click on Add after selecting Client Categories to create a new category.

It is used to group clients for the purpose of Front and back load charges. Different Front and back load charges can be applied to different groups of client. Some groups of clients can be exempted altogether from such charges.

Click on Add after selecting Client Groups to set up a new group.

This is used to setup agencies of the fund management company to enable the system to know which clients came through which agency.

Click on Add after selecting Agencies to create a new agency setup.

Important: Master setup changes require proper authorization before becoming active in the system.

Customer Management

Customer Relationship Management

Comprehensive customer management from initial setup through ongoing relationship maintenance.

Steps:

  • Click on new customer
  • Choose account type and branch
  • Click on proceed to fill the KYC FORM
  • After making the required entries
  • Check the Submit to checker button (if you are sure of the entries made)
  • Click on save. (details will be pending approval for the authorizer to approve)

Steps:

  • Click on customer search
  • Type a search string or parameter to limit the search results
  • Select Search

Authorizations

Authorization Workflow

The CRM module implements a multi-level authorization workflow for critical operations. Used to review records that have been submitted for approval in this module. An approver can approve or reject a record with a stated reason for the rejection. The record is live only after approval. A rejected record returns to the Pending Submissions module for the inputter to review and re-submit or delete.

All customer setups that were submitted to checker will be pending approval or rejection here.

For Customer Setups, Click on preview to view customer details...

After viewing customer details, check the box to approve or reject. Then click Approve to approve or reject to reject. Select Save to proceed.

All CIS account setups that were submitted to checker will be pending approval or rejection here.

Follow the same approval process as customer setups - preview, check appropriate box, and save your decision.

All portfolio account setups that were submitted to checker will be pending approval or rejection here.

Review account details and make authorization decision following the standard workflow.

All PF account setups that were submitted to checker will be pending approval or rejection here.

All bulk account upload files that were submitted to checker will be pending approval or rejection here.

Authorization Required For: New customer setups, account modifications, financial transactions, and compliance-related changes.
Authorization Workflow Steps
1 Submission: User submits item for authorization
2 Review: Authorizer reviews the submission details
3 Decision: Approve, reject, or request modifications
4 Notification: Original submitter receives status update

Compliance Review

Regulatory Compliance Checks

The CRM system includes compliance checks for both CIS and Portfolio accounts to ensure regulatory requirements are met. This is used by the compliance department to review all approved client account Setups, where applicable. Compliance officers will have a detailed view of the account and can approve or reject the account.

Review pending CIS accounts for regulatory compliance before activation.

  • KYC verification
  • Risk assessment
  • Documentation review

Verify portfolio accounts meet investment guidelines and regulations.

  • Investment suitability
  • Client risk profile alignment
  • Regulatory documentation
Compliance Workflow: Items failing compliance checks will be returned to the originator with detailed feedback for correction.
