Collective Investment Scheme Overview

Welcome to Norbus e-Purse CIS Module

A comprehensive Mutual Fund/Unit Trust Management Module. Together with the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and Cashier modules, the CIS module provides solutions to manage collective Investment Schemes whose underlying assets are managed with E-purse Asset Manager (AM) and Mutual Funds whose assets are not managed or Linked to the E-purse Asset Manager (AM) module.

The CIS module handles all fund-related operations including:

  • Fund Setup and Configuration
  • Net Asset Value (NAV) Calculation
  • Purchase and Redemption Processing
  • Fund Distribution Management
  • Reconciliation and Reporting
  • Multi-level Authorization Workflow
  • Real-time Notifications and Alerts
Master Setups

Configure fund types, unit classes, and system parameters

CIS Products

Create and manage collective investment schemes

Umbrella Structure

Manage umbrella funds and sub-fund structures

NAV Processing

Calculate and manage net asset values

Transactions

Process transfers, switches, and mergers

Manage dividend and income distributions

Master Setups

Fund Configuration

This is used to perform Setups that are required during the main Fund/Unit Trust Setup and Management pages. These Setups will drop down for selection in subsequent pages within the module.

This is used to set up the various types of Mutual funds/collective funds as determined by the Fund Management Company. It allows Funds with similar characteristics to be grouped under a Fund Type. This setup will show as a dropdown at Fund Setup.

Clicking on set up new fund type allows you to add a new fund type.

This is used to set up Fund member groups for Management Fees. Different groups of clients can be charged different Management Fees. Client Share Classes will show as a dropdown at Fund Fee Setup. Existing Funds can also be assigned to a client class at this setup. The list of all active client classes can be viewed and updated.

Clicking on set up new unit class allows you to create a new unit class.

NAV Operations

Net Asset Value Processing

Daily calculation and management of fund Net Asset Values, including fee processing and performance tracking. / This menu is used for Fund NAV computation and fees processing.

Used for Fund Net Asset Valuation computation. You’ll be required to choose the Fund and the valuation date. E-purse will check if NAV has not been done for the selected Fund and date before proceeding to compute the NAV. For Funds linked to e-purse AM (portfolio) account, Gross-Asset-valuation of the fund must be done for the selected date in the Asset Manager (AM) module. For Funds not linked to AM, users will be required to input the Gross-AUM for the selected date.


Clicking on run NAV allows you to initiate the NAV calculation process.


Click on fetch after filling the form with required details.

This is used to process accrued Fund Fees. NAV-based fees are charged at every NAV computation and accrued, allowing the Fund Manager to choose when to make the debit entry. You’ll be required to select the Fund and the accrual dates for E-purse to fetch the fees charged for the period. The Fund manager can then choose the bank account to debit, the date to record the debit, and whether to record the fee amount per NAV, or the total Fee amount for the selected period.


Clicking on post NAV fees allows you to process fees for the calculated NAV.


Click on fetch after entering the required details.

NAV Calculation Components
1 Asset Valuation
2 Liability Calculation
3 Unit Reconciliation
4 Fee Processing

Transactions

Investor Transactions

Manage investor transactions including purchases, redemptions, and transfers with comprehensive tracking and validation. / This menu is used for transferring funds and merging transactions and clients’ data.

Used to transfer funds from one client to another in the same collective/mutual fund. You’ll be required to choose the Fund within which the transfer is to be made, the transfer date, the clients from which funds will be debited and the client to which funds will be credited. You can transfer cash amounts or shares/units, that do not exceed the displayed client’s balance as at the transfer date.

Clicking on save after filling the form. Remember to submit to checker.

Used for interfund transfers. You can transfer a cash amount from client A in mutual fund A to client B in mutual fund B using Switch, if mutual funds A and B exist in the e-purse.

Click on save after filling the form. Remeber to submit to checker

This is used to consolidate transactions or join two client’s accounts in the same mutual fund. Users can:
1. Merge all transactions and bio-data of client A to client B in mutual fund AB, in which case e-purse will disable client A from the Fund.
2. Merge all transactions of client A to client B in mutual fund AB, within a selected period.
3. Merge some selected transactions of client A to client B in mutual fund AB, within a selected period.


Clicking on merge allows you to initiate a merge transaction. Just like the previous procedures, fill out the form, submit to checker and click on save to proceed.

Reconciliation

Daily Reconciliation Processes

Ensure accuracy and completeness of all transactions through systematic reconciliation of purchases, receipts, and bank balances. / This is used to reconcile verified receipts/deposits from the Fund's ‘suspense’ account to clients. E-purse automatically creates a ‘suspense account’ in every existing mutual Fund where receipts that cannot be immediately assigned to clients can be kept temporarily. The receipts can then be verified and used for purchases and later reconciled with the client’s accounts.

Used for reconciling purchased/subscribed receipts from the suspense account to a client. / Reconcile purchase applications against received funds to ensure all transactions are accurately recorded.

Click on Fetch after choosing fund for the needed details.

Used for reconciling verified receipts in the suspense accounts that are pending purchase. / Verify that all investor payments have been received and reconciled against system records.


The procedure for the Purshase From Suspense above, applies here as well.

For reconciling transactions in T24 with those of the E-purse. / Reconcile system records with bank statements to ensure all transactions are accurately reflected in financial records.

Click on fetch after choosing fund. This allows you to initiate the reconciliation process for bank transactions.


Click on upload to upload Cash Reconciliation

Daily Requirement: All reconciliations must be completed before end of business day to ensure accurate NAV calculations.

Fund Distribution

Dividend and Income Distribution

Process fund distributions to investors including dividends, capital gains, and other income allocations according to fund policies. / This is used to process the distribution of gains made by the fund's holdings, pro rata to clients. E-purse offers multiple distribution methodologies including distribution of returns with/without price reset, distribution of income and dividends realized, distribution of capital gains.

This page displays all distribution schemes due for processing. It is tied to Fund Setup and Distribution Dates Setup. For existing mutual Funds which have been setup to distribute gains and for which distribution schedules have been setup, you’ll be required to search the fund for due distributions and choose the Fund bank account to process gain distribution. You’ll see a notification for Fund gain distributions that are due at the CIS homepage


Click on fetch after choosing fund. This allows you to initiate the reconciliation process for bank transactions.


Now after a result has been displayed below, select the fund of choice, choose bank code and click on process to process distribution or reject to reject Distribution

Unsubmitted Items

Pending Submissions

This is for holding data that is yet to be submitted for approval. Saving a Master Setup or a Transaction or NAV computation in MF without checking the “Submit to checker” button, or an approver rejecting a setup or Transaction, will send the data to this container. Users can then view/modify/delete and/or submit the data to the approver from this item.

All changes or updates that were saved without ticking the submit to checker button will be pending submission here.

All changes made will remain in the Unsubmitted items list Until sumbmitted to Checker

All transactions that were saved without ticking the submit to checker button will be pending submission here.

Click on the Fetch button after choosing product and any unsubmitted item under that product will be displayed.


The displayed Item/Product, can either be editted, delete or can be submitted for review by checking the Item/Items and click the Submit checked boxes button to proceed.

All NAV calculations that were saved without ticking the submit to checker button will be pending submission here.


Click on the Fetch button after choosing fund and selecting date as at and any NAV pending submission as at the selected date will be displayed.


The displayed Item, can either be editted, deleted or can be submitted for review by checking the Item/Items and clicking the Submit checked boxes button to proceed.

All master setups that were saved without ticking the submit to checker button will be pending submission here.


When an Item is displayed here, you can either edit or delete or submit to checker by checking the Item/Items and click the Submit checked boxes button to proceed, as in the case of other modules.

As in the case of the Master Setups,all umbrella CIS and CIS Product setups that were saved without ticking the submit to checker button will be pending submission on their pages respectively.


Please refer to the Master Setups Unsubmitted items and apply the same procedures in submitting an Item for review
