GL Overview

Welcome to Norbus e-Purse Fund Accounting (General Ledger) Module

The Fund Accounting (General Ledger) module provides comprehensive financial accounting capabilities for fund management operations. This module handles all aspects of general ledger management, from chart of accounts setup to transaction processing and financial reporting.

The Fund Accounting module provides complete functionality for:

  • Account group hierarchy management
  • Chart of accounts configuration
  • Journal entry processing and approval
  • End-of-year processing
  • Transaction views and reporting
  • Comprehensive audit trails
  • Financial controls and approvals

Getting Started with Fund Accounting (GL)
1 Account Structure: Set up main groups and sub-groups to organize your chart of accounts.
2 Chart of Accounts: Create GL accounts and configure your complete chart of accounts.
3 Journal Entries: Process daily transactions through the journal entry system.
4 Approvals: Ensure all transactions are properly reviewed and approved.
Best Practice: Always maintain proper segregation of duties with maker-checker controls.
