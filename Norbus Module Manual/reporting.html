Reporting Module Overview

Welcome to Norbus e-Purse Reporting Module

The Reporting Module provides comprehensive reporting capabilities across all system modules, enabling stakeholders to access critical business information, regulatory reports, and operational insights. This module centralizes all reporting functions for efficient data analysis and decision-making.

The Reporting Module delivers complete functionality for:

  • Customer relationship management reports
  • Asset management and portfolio analytics
  • Collective investment scheme reporting
  • Fund administration and member reports
  • Financial accounting and GL reports
  • Regulatory compliance reporting
  • Comprehensive audit trails and logs

Getting Started with Reporting
1 Access Control: Ensure appropriate permissions are granted for accessing specific report categories.
2 Report Selection: Navigate to the relevant report category based on your requirements.
3 Parameter Setup: Configure report parameters including date ranges, filters, and output formats.
4 Generation & Export: Generate reports and export in desired formats (PDF, Excel, CSV).
Best Practice: Schedule regular reports for automated delivery to stakeholders.
