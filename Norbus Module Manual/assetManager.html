Asset Manager Module Overview

Welcome to Norbus e-Purse Asset Manager Module

The Asset Manager module is designed to streamline the management of mutual funds, collective investment schemes, and related operations. It provides a comprehensive suite of tools for fund setup, NAV processing, transaction management, and compliance controls.

This module is essential for financial institutions managing mutual funds, enabling efficient operations, regulatory compliance, and enhanced customer service.

System Access Requirements
1 Login Credentials: Ensure you have valid username and password provided by your system administrator.
2 Browser Requirements: Use a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) with JavaScript enabled.
3 Network Access: Ensure stable internet connection and access to the organization's network.
4 User Permissions: Your access level determines which features are available to you.
Security Note: Always log out when finished and never share your login credentials.

Securities Management

Securities Configuration Options

Securities management allows you to configure and manage all aspects of financial instruments. You can set up asset industries, register new securities, monitor prices, and manage bond schedules for comprehensive portfolio and investment tracking.

Asset Industries allows you to manage and categorize securities by industry sectors. You can create industry classifications, organize securities by market sectors, and maintain structured categorization for better portfolio analysis and reporting.

Clicking on Add Industry allows you to create a new industry category

while clicking on the Edit button allows you to modify existing industry classifications.

New Security Registration allows you to register and configure new financial instruments in the system. You can set up stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and other securities with detailed specifications, pricing information, and associated metadata for comprehensive tracking.

The security setup form allows you to configure all essential details including security type, industry classification, pricing parameters, and trading specifications.

Search Securities allows you to find and view existing securities in your portfolio. You can filter by various criteria, view detailed security information, and access performance metrics and historical data for informed decision-making.

The search interface provides advanced filtering options to quickly locate specific securities based on symbol, name, industry, or other criteria.

Security Prices Monitoring allows you to access real-time and historical pricing data for all listed securities. You can track price movements, analyze trends, and monitor market performance to make informed investment decisions.

The pricing dashboard displays current market values, price changes, and performance indicators for comprehensive market analysis.

Bonds Coupon Schedule allows you to manage and track coupon payment dates for bond investments. You can view upcoming payment schedules, historical coupon payments, and ensure proper income tracking for fixed-income securities in your portfolio.

The coupon schedule interface displays payment dates, amounts, and status for all bond holdings, helping you track income from fixed-income investments.

Corporate Actions

Corporate Action Management

Manage corporate actions including dividend declarations, stock splits, bonus issues, and other corporate events that affect shareholder equity and investment portfolios.

Set up and manage dividend declarations for corporate securities, including dividend amounts, record dates, payment dates, and eligibility criteria for shareholders.

Clicking on Declare Dividend allows you to initiate a new dividend declaration process.

The dividend declaration form allows you to specify dividend rates, payment schedules, and shareholder eligibility requirements.

Investment Management

Investment Portfolio Management

Investment Management allows you to handle all aspects of investment operations including booking new investments, managing sales, processing maturities, and tracking active portfolios. You can execute transactions, monitor performance, and manage cash flows for comprehensive investment administration.

Book Investment allows you to record new investment purchases and add securities to client portfolios. You can specify investment details, quantities, prices, and settlement information to properly document all investment transactions in the system.

Clicking on New Investment allows you to book a new investment transaction

while the booking form allows you to enter all necessary investment details including security selection, quantity, pricing, and client allocation information.

Sell/Deliver Investment allows you to process investment sales and deliveries from client portfolios. You can execute partial or full sales, calculate gains/losses, and manage settlement procedures for investment disposals and transfers.

Clicking on Sell Investment allows you to initiate a sale transaction

while the sale interface allows you to specify quantities, pricing, and settlement terms for the disposal.

Post From Suspense allows you to transfer cash from suspense accounts to proper investment accounts. You can allocate unidentified payments, resolve pending transactions, and ensure accurate cash positioning across all investment portfolios.

The suspense management interface displays pending cash items awaiting proper allocation.

Running Investments displays all active investment positions in client portfolios. You can monitor current holdings, track performance, view valuations, and access detailed investment information for ongoing portfolio management and reporting.

The active investments dashboard provides comprehensive views of all current holdings and their performance metrics.

Post Matured Investments allows you to process investment maturities and convert securities to cash. You can handle automatic redemptions, process principal repayments, and manage the settlement of matured fixed-income and other time-bound investments.

Clicking on Process Maturity allows you to convert matured investments to cash

Post Matured Coupons allows you to process coupon payments from bond investments and convert them to cash. You can handle periodic interest payments, manage coupon collection schedules, and ensure accurate income distribution to client accounts.

The coupon processing interface displays all pending coupon payments ready for collection and posting.

Purchase Simulation allows you to model and analyze potential investment purchases before execution. You can calculate costs, fees, yields, and projected returns to help clients make informed investment decisions and optimize portfolio allocations.

Clicking on New Simulation allows you to create a new investment scenario analysis

while the simulation results provide detailed projections and analysis for investment decision-making.

General Transactions

Transaction Management System

General Transactions allows you to manage various financial transactions including general ledger entries, fund transfers, transaction merging, and fee computations. You can process diverse transaction types, handle inter-bank transfers, and manage complex transaction workflows for comprehensive financial operations.

General Transactions allows you to record various financial transactions that don't fall into specific categories. You can capture miscellaneous entries, adjustments, corrections, and other general ledger transactions with flexible account mapping and detailed documentation.

Clicking on New Transaction allows you to create a new general transaction entry

while the transaction form allows you to specify accounts, amounts, descriptions, and supporting documentation.

Funds Transfer allows you to process inter-bank transfers and move funds between different financial institutions. You can initiate wire transfers, electronic funds transfers, and other payment methods while managing routing information, fees, and settlement procedures.

Clicking on New Transfer allows you to initiate an inter-bank funds transfer

while the transfer interface allows you to specify beneficiary details, routing information, and transfer amounts.

Merge Transactions allows you to combine multiple related transactions into single consolidated entries. You can merge duplicate entries, consolidate partial payments, and streamline transaction records for cleaner accounting and improved reporting accuracy.

Clicking on Select Transactions allows you to choose transactions for merging

while the merge process allows you to review, validate, and combine selected transactions into a unified record.

Compute Management Fees allows you to calculate and process management fees based on asset values and fee structures. You can apply different fee rates, handle tiered pricing, compute performance fees, and generate automatic fee entries for accurate revenue recognition and client billing.

Clicking on Calculate Fees allows you to compute management fees for selected periods

while the fee computation engine applies rate structures, calculates amounts, and generates transaction entries.

Uploads

Bulk Data Upload Management

Uploads allows you to import large volumes of data efficiently through file-based uploads. You can upload investment transactions, sales records, security prices, and exchange rates in bulk, with approval workflows and validation processes to ensure data accuracy and system integrity.

Upload Investments allows you to import multiple investment transactions from spreadsheets or data files. You can process bulk investment purchases, validate data formats, and prepare transactions for approval workflows to streamline large-scale investment processing operations.

Clicking on Choose File allows you to select an investment data file for upload

while the validation process checks data integrity and displays any errors or warnings before processing.

Submit Uploaded Investments allows you to review and approve investment uploads before final processing. You can validate uploaded data, make corrections if needed, and submit approved transactions to the main system for official recording and settlement.

Clicking on Review Upload allows you to examine uploaded investments before approval

while the approval workflow ensures all transactions meet compliance requirements before final submission.

Upload Investment Sales allows you to import multiple investment sale transactions from external files. You can process bulk disposals, validate sale details, and prepare sales transactions for approval workflows to efficiently manage large-scale portfolio rebalancing operations.

Clicking on Upload Sales File allows you to import investment sale transactions

while the system validates sale details including security holdings, quantities, and pricing information.

Submit Uploaded Sales allows you to review and approve investment sales uploads before execution. You can verify sale details, check portfolio positions, and submit approved sales transactions to the trading system for processing and settlement.

The approval dashboard displays all pending sales uploads awaiting review and authorization.

while the review process ensures all sales comply with portfolio guidelines and regulatory requirements.

Uploaded Security Prices allows you to import current market prices for securities from external data sources. You can update portfolio valuations, process daily pricing feeds, and maintain accurate market values for comprehensive portfolio management and reporting.

Clicking on Upload Prices allows you to import security pricing data files

while the pricing engine validates and applies new prices to update portfolio valuations automatically.

Uploaded Exchange Rates allows you to import foreign exchange rates from external sources for multi-currency operations. You can update currency conversion rates, process daily FX feeds, and maintain accurate cross-currency valuations for international investment portfolios.

Clicking on Upload Rates allows you to import foreign exchange rate data

while the system validates rates against historical data and applies updates to multi-currency portfolios.

Internal Controls

Risk Management & Compliance Controls

Internal Controls allows you to manage risk parameters and compliance limits for investment portfolios. You can set asset allocation limits, configure issuer exposure limits, and define threshold parameters to ensure portfolios remain within acceptable risk boundaries and regulatory requirements.

Asset Class Allocations allows you to define and monitor asset allocation limits across different investment categories. You can set minimum and maximum allocation percentages, track current allocations, and receive alerts when portfolios deviate from strategic asset allocation targets.

Clicking on Set Limits allows you to configure asset class allocation parameters

while the monitoring system tracks actual vs. target allocations and generates compliance reports.

Issuer Limits allows you to manage concentration risk by setting exposure limits for individual issuers and counterparties. You can define maximum exposure percentages, monitor current holdings, and prevent excessive concentration in any single issuer to maintain portfolio diversification.

The issuer limits interface allows you to set and monitor concentration limits by issuer.

Thresholds allows you to define warning and breach levels for various portfolio metrics and risk parameters. You can set threshold values for volatility, duration, credit quality, and other risk measures to receive timely alerts and maintain portfolios within acceptable risk tolerance levels.

Clicking on Set Thresholds allows you to configure warning and breach levels

Reversals

Transaction Reversal Management

Reversals allows you to undo or correct previously processed transactions and operations. You can reverse various types of transactions including valuations, investments, pricing updates, and corporate actions to maintain data integrity and correct processing errors when necessary.

Reverse Portfolio Valuation allows you to undo previously processed portfolio valuations. You can reverse incorrect valuations, revert pricing updates, and restore previous valuation states to correct errors and maintain accurate portfolio records.

Select the valuation date and confirm the reversal to undo the portfolio valuation process.

Reverse Exchange Rates allows you to undo foreign exchange rate updates and restore previous rate values. You can correct erroneous rate entries, revert automated updates, and maintain accurate currency conversion rates.

Select the rate update to reverse and restore previous exchange rate values.

Reverse Security Prices allows you to undo security price updates and restore previous pricing data. You can correct pricing errors, revert bulk price updates, and maintain accurate security valuations.

Select the price update to reverse and restore previous security pricing information.

Additional reversal options include reversing investments/sales, general transactions, payment-in-kind distributions, posted dividends, share splits, management fees, maturity postings, and suspense account transfers. Each reversal type maintains audit trails and requires proper authorization for data integrity.

Authorization Reversal

Reversal Authorization & Approval

Authorization Reversal allows you to review and approve reversal requests before they are executed. You can examine reversal justifications, validate reversal impacts, and provide authorization approval to ensure proper controls and audit trails for all reversal operations in the system.

Authorize Portfolio Valuation Reversal allows you to review and approve requests to reverse portfolio valuations. You can examine the reversal rationale, assess impact on dependent processes, and provide authorization to ensure proper oversight of valuation reversal operations.

Review pending valuation reversal requests and provide authorization approval or rejection.

All Authorization Requests provides a comprehensive view of pending reversal authorizations across all transaction types. You can review requests for exchange rate reversals, security price reversals, investment reversals, general transaction reversals, dividend reversals, share split reversals, management fee reversals, and maturity reversal authorizations in one centralized interface.

The authorization dashboard displays all pending requests with priority indicators and approval workflows.
