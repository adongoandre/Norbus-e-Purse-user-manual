CRM Overview

Welcome to Norbus e-Purse CRM

A one-stop Customer Relationship Management module for the entire system. The Client Relationship Management (CRM) system is a comprehensive platform designed to manage customer relationships, account setups, and financial services within the Norbus e-Purse ecosystem. This system provides tools for customer onboarding, account management, and compliance monitoring.

The CRM module handles all customer-related operations including:

  • Onboarding New Clients
  • Search functionalities
  • Checking of investment balances
  • Viewing of Statements
  • Maintenance of existing client’s information
  • Uploading of KYC documents
  • Customer collateral setups
  • Set Post-No-Debit (PND) on customers
  • Tagging of deceased clients
  • Setting up of Leads

Master Setups

Configuration Management

The CRM has a master setup that is used to perform setups of relevant information required for the main customer setup and maintenance pages. These master setups show as drop-downs for selection in the client setup / maintenance pages. Master setups include:

People Management
  • Relationship Managers: This is used to setup/add RMs under which clients will be assigned. Details of existing RMs could also be edited or deleted.
  • Titles: To setup all titles that will be needed during client creation. Eg. Mr. Mrs.
Organizational Structure
  • Client Categories: For adding new client categories, editing and deleting existing ones. This is tied to the Regulatory Bodies' setup. Every client falls under a client category which in turn falls under a regulatory body.
  • Client Groups: It is used to group clients for the purposed of Front and back load charges. Different Front and back load charges can be applied to different groups of client. Some groups of clients can be exempted altogether from such charges.
  • Agencies: This is used to setup agencies of the fund management company to enable the system to know which clients came through which agency.

To Add a Setup:

Click on Add after you have selected what you want to setup.

Eg.


Fill in the required information, submit to checker and save.


Important: Master setup changes require proper authorization before becoming active in the system.

Authorizations

Authorization Workflow
Regulatory Authorization Workflows

The CRM module implements a multi-level authorization workflow for critical operations. Used to review records that have been submitted for approval in this module. An approver can approve or reject a record with a stated reason for the rejection. The record is live only after approval. A rejected record returns to the Pending Submissions module for the inputter to review and re-submit or delete. Items requiring authorization will appear in the respective pending review sections.

Authorization Required For: New customer setups, account modifications, financial transactions, and compliance-related changes.
Authorization Sections include:
Customer Setups

New customers and profile modifications

CIS Accounts

New accounts and services

Portfolio Accounts

Account setups and services

PF Accounts

New accounts and bulk uploads

Account Uploads

Bulk account creation files

Eg.

For Customer Setups, Click on preview to view customer details...

Now, after viewing customer details, check the box to approve or reject. Then click Approve to approve or reject to reject. Select Save to proceed.

1 Submission: User submits item for authorization
2 Review: Authorizer reviews the submission details
3 Decision: Approve, reject, or request modifications
4 Notification: Original submitter receives status update

Compliance Review

Regulatory Compliance Checks

The CRM system includes compliance checks for both CIS and Portfolio accounts to ensure regulatory requirements are met. This is used by the compliance department to review all approved client account Setups, where applicable. Compliance officers will have a detailed view of the account and can approve or reject the account.

CIS Account Compliance

Review pending CIS accounts for regulatory compliance before activation.

  • KYC verification
  • Risk assessment
  • Documentation review
Portfolio Account Compliance

Verify portfolio accounts meet investment guidelines and regulations.

  • Investment suitability
  • Client risk profile alignment
  • Regulatory documentation
Compliance Workflow: Items failing compliance checks will be returned to the originator with detailed feedback for correction.

Unsubmitted Items

Pending Submissions

This is used for viewing/modifying and/or submitting records for approval. Every Master Setup and Transactional record in this module that was saved without ticking the Submit to Checker button will be saved here. The Inputter can then view, edit, delete or submit the record for approval at his/her convenience. Track and manage items that have been started but not yet submitted for authorization.

Submenus are:

  • Master Setups
  • Customers
  • CIS Accounts
  • CIS Account Services
  • Portfolio Accounts
  • Portfolio Accounts Services
  • PF Accounts

Eg.

For "Master Setups", You can either add new record, edit, or delete.

Else, check the box you want to submit and and click submit checked boxes


Note: This applies to the other Submenus as well.

Reminder: Unsubmitted items will not appear in authorization queues until formally submitted.

Troubleshooting & Support

Common Issues and Solutions

Forgotten Password: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to reset your credentials.
Account Locked: Contact your system administrator to unlock your account after multiple failed attempts.

Missing Fields: The system highlights required fields that need completion before submission.
Validation Errors: Review error messages and correct the indicated data issues.

Pending Authorization: Check the authorization queue for your item's status.
Returned Items: Review comments from the authorizer for required corrections.
