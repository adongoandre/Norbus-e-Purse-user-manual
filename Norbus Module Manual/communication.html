Communication Module Overview

Welcome to Norbus e-Purse Communication (CRM) Module

The Communication (CRM) module provides comprehensive SMS and Email communication capabilities for customer relationship management. This module enables targeted communication across all system modules including CIS, Portfolio, and Provident Fund operations.

The Communication module provides complete functionality for:

  • Customer SMS broadcasting and file-based messaging
  • System-generated SMS notifications for all modules
  • Customer email campaigns and targeted messaging
  • Automated email notifications for system events
  • Comprehensive communication logs and audit trails
  • Event-driven communication triggers
  • Multi-module integration support

Getting Started with Communication (CRM)
1 Configure Templates: Set up SMS and email templates for consistent messaging.

Customer SMS

Customer SMS Communication Management

SMS Communication

Broadcast SMS enables you to send mass SMS messages to all customers or selected customer groups for general announcements, promotions, and important updates.

Broadcast Features:
  • Mass Messaging: Send SMS to all customers or filtered groups
  • Template Support: Use pre-defined templates for consistent messaging
  • Personalization: Include customer-specific information in messages
  • Scheduling: Schedule SMS delivery for optimal timing
  • Delivery Tracking: Monitor delivery status and success rates
Important: Ensure compliance with SMS marketing regulations and opt-out requirements.

File-based SMS

Contact From File allows you to upload customer contact lists from external files and send targeted SMS messages to specific customer segments.

File-based Communication:
  • File Upload: Support for CSV, Excel, and text file formats
  • Data Validation: Automatic validation of phone numbers and customer data
  • Targeted Messaging: Send messages to specific customer lists
  • Batch Processing: Handle large customer lists efficiently
  • Error Handling: Report and manage invalid contacts
Supported Formats: CSV, Excel (.xlsx, .xls), and comma-separated text files.

System SMS Notifications

Automated System SMS Management

CIS Module

CIS SMS Notifications provide automated messaging for Customer Information System events and operations, keeping customers informed about their account status and activities.

CIS Communication Types:
  • General Broadcast: System-wide announcements to all CIS users
  • Operational SMS: Transaction confirmations, account updates, and system notifications
  • On Events: Triggered messages for account opening, closures, and status changes

Portfolio Module

Portfolio SMS Notifications deliver investment-related communications including portfolio updates, market alerts, and transaction confirmations.

Portfolio Communication Types:
  • General Broadcast: Market updates and portfolio announcements
  • Operational SMS: Trade confirmations, settlement notices, and portfolio changes
  • On Events: NAV updates, dividend distributions, and corporate actions

Provident Fund Module

PF SMS Notifications handle provident fund-related communications including contribution confirmations, benefit statements, and withdrawal notifications.

PF Communication Types:
  • General Broadcast: Fund performance updates and scheme announcements
  • Operational SMS: Contribution receipts, benefit calculations, and account updates
  • On Events: Vesting milestones, withdrawal approvals, and scheme transfers

Customer Email

Customer Email Communication Management

Email Communication

Broadcast Email provides comprehensive email marketing and communication capabilities for sending newsletters, announcements, and promotional content to customers.

Email Broadcast Features:
  • Rich Content: HTML email templates with images, formatting, and branding
  • Mass Distribution: Send to all customers or segmented groups
  • Personalization: Dynamic content based on customer data
  • A/B Testing: Test different email versions for optimization
  • Delivery Analytics: Track open rates, click-through rates, and engagement
Analytics: Comprehensive email performance tracking and reporting available.

File-based Email

Contact From File enables targeted email campaigns using external customer lists and contact databases for precise customer segmentation.

File-based Email Features:
  • Multiple Formats: Support for CSV, Excel, and various data formats
  • Email Validation: Automatic validation of email addresses
  • Custom Fields: Map file columns to personalization fields
  • Bounce Management: Handle invalid emails and bounces automatically
  • Unsubscribe Handling: Respect opt-out preferences and manage suppression lists
Privacy: Ensure GDPR compliance and customer consent before using external lists.

System Email Notifications

Automated System Email Management

CIS Module

CIS Email Notifications provide comprehensive email communications for Customer Information System events, including detailed account statements and documentation.

CIS Email Communication Types:
  • General Broadcast: System announcements and policy updates via email
  • Operational Email: Account statements, transaction confirmations, and notices
  • On Events: Welcome emails, account activation confirmations, and status updates
Attachments: Support for PDF statements, documents, and regulatory notices.

Portfolio Module

Portfolio Email Notifications deliver detailed investment communications including portfolio reports, market analysis, and performance summaries.

Portfolio Email Communication Types:
  • General Broadcast: Market insights, investment newsletters, and portfolio updates
  • Operational Email: Trade confirmations, settlement notices, and portfolio valuations
  • On Events: NAV reports, dividend statements, and corporate action notices
Rich Reports: Detailed portfolio analytics and performance charts included.

Provident Fund Module

PF Email Notifications handle comprehensive provident fund communications including annual statements, benefit projections, and regulatory compliance documents.

PF Email Communication Types:
  • General Broadcast: Annual fund reports, scheme performance, and regulatory updates
  • Operational Email: Contribution statements, benefit calculations, and account summaries
  • On Events: Vesting notifications, withdrawal confirmations, and transfer certificates
Compliance: All PF communications must meet regulatory disclosure requirements.

Logs & Reports

Communication Tracking and Audit

SMS Audit Trail

SMS Logs provide comprehensive tracking and audit capabilities for all SMS communications sent through the system, ensuring compliance and delivery monitoring.

SMS Log Features:
  • Delivery Status: Track sent, delivered, failed, and pending SMS messages
  • Recipient Details: Complete log of recipients, phone numbers, and customer IDs
  • Message Content: Archive of all SMS content sent through the system
  • Time Stamps: Precise timing of SMS creation, sending, and delivery
  • Cost Tracking: Monitor SMS usage and associated costs
  • Export Options: Download logs for external analysis and reporting
Search & Filter: Advanced filtering by date, recipient, message type, and delivery status.

Email Audit Trail

Email Logs maintain detailed records of all email communications, including delivery statistics, engagement metrics, and compliance documentation.

Email Log Features:
  • Delivery Analytics: Track opens, clicks, bounces, and unsubscribes
  • Campaign Performance: Detailed metrics for broadcast and targeted campaigns
  • Content Archive: Complete email content and template history
  • Recipient Management: Track email preferences and opt-out status
  • Error Reporting: Identify and resolve email delivery issues
  • Compliance Records: Maintain records for regulatory requirements
Performance Metrics: Comprehensive email marketing analytics and ROI tracking.

Available Reports:
1 Daily Communication Summary: Overview of all SMS and email activities
2 Module-wise Communication: Breakdown by CIS, Portfolio, and PF modules
3 Delivery Performance Report: Success rates, failures, and optimization insights
4 Customer Engagement Analysis: Response rates and communication preferences
Report Features:
  • Scheduled Reports: Automated daily, weekly, and monthly reports
  • Custom Date Ranges: Generate reports for specific time periods
  • Export Formats: PDF, Excel, and CSV export options
  • Dashboard Integration: Real-time communication metrics
