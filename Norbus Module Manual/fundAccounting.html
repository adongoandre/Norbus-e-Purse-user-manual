Account Groups

Account Hierarchy Management

Main Groups represent the highest level of account organization in your chart of accounts. They provide the primary categorization for all financial accounts.

Main Group Features:
  • Account Categories: Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income, and Expenses
  • Hierarchical Structure: Top-level organization for account grouping
  • Reporting Foundation: Forms the basis for financial statement preparation
  • Control Totals: Enables summary-level financial controls and validation
Note: Main groups should align with your organization's financial reporting structure.

Sub Groups provide detailed categorization within main groups, allowing for more granular organization and reporting of accounts.

Sub Group Configuration:
  • Detailed Classification: Further breakdown of main group categories
  • Flexible Structure: Create multiple levels of sub-categorization
  • Enhanced Reporting: Support for detailed financial analysis and reporting
  • Account Organization: Logical grouping for related accounts
Best Practice: Design sub-groups to support your reporting and analysis needs.

Accounts

General Ledger Account Management

GL Account management allows you to create and maintain individual general ledger accounts that will be used for recording all financial transactions.

Account Management Features:
  • Account Creation: Set up new GL accounts with unique account codes
  • Account Properties: Configure account type, normal balance, and status
  • Account Mapping: Link accounts to appropriate account groups
  • Control Settings: Set up posting controls and restrictions
Important: Account codes should follow your organization's numbering convention.

Chart of Account (COA) provides a comprehensive view of your complete account structure, showing the relationship between main groups, sub-groups, and individual accounts.

COA Management:
  • Complete Overview: View entire account structure in hierarchical format
  • Account Relationships: Understand parent-child relationships between accounts
  • Maintenance Tools: Bulk updates and account structure modifications
  • Validation: Ensure account structure integrity and completeness
Tip: Use the COA view to validate your account structure before going live.

Transactions

Financial Transaction Processing

Journal Entry is the primary method for recording financial transactions in the general ledger. All accounting entries follow the double-entry bookkeeping principle.

Journal Entry Features:
  • Double Entry: Automatic validation of debit and credit balance
  • Multi-Line Entries: Support for complex transactions with multiple accounts
  • Reference Management: Link entries to source documents and references
  • Validation Controls: Ensure accuracy and completeness before posting
Principle: All journal entries must balance (Total Debits = Total Credits).

Generate EOY (End of Year) processing handles year-end closing procedures, including profit/loss transfers and account balances preparation for the new fiscal year.

EOY Processing:
  • Closing Entries: Automatic generation of year-end closing journal entries
  • P&L Transfer: Transfer profit and loss to retained earnings
  • Balance Validation: Ensure all temporary accounts are properly closed
  • New Year Setup: Prepare account balances for new fiscal year
Caution: EOY processing should be performed only after all year-end transactions are complete.

Views & Reports

Transaction Views and Financial Reports

All Transactions provides a comprehensive view of all financial transactions recorded in the general ledger, with powerful filtering and search capabilities.

Transaction View Features:
  • Complete History: View all posted transactions across all accounts
  • Advanced Filtering: Filter by date range, account, amount, or reference
  • Search Functionality: Quick search across transaction descriptions and references
  • Export Options: Export transaction data to Excel or PDF formats

Journal Entries view provides a focused look at manual journal entries, showing the complete audit trail of all accounting adjustments and manual postings.

Journal Entry View:
  • Entry Details: View complete journal entry with all debit and credit lines
  • Approval Status: Track approval workflow and authorization status
  • Audit Trail: Complete history of entry creation and modifications
  • Reference Links: Access supporting documents and references
Note: This view excludes system-generated automated entries for clarity.

Approvals

Journal Entry Approval Workflow

Approve Journal Entries manages the approval workflow for all journal entries that have been submitted for review. This ensures proper oversight and financial control.

Approval Process Features:
  • Pending Queue: View all journal entries awaiting approval
  • Review Tools: Detailed review interface with supporting documents
  • Approval Actions: Approve, reject, or return entries for modification
  • Bulk Processing: Approve multiple entries simultaneously when appropriate
Control: Maker-checker approval ensures financial integrity and compliance.

Standard Approval Process:
1 Entry Creation: User creates journal entry and submits for approval
2 Review Queue: Entry appears in supervisor's approval queue
3 Approval Decision: Supervisor approves, rejects, or requests modifications
4 Posting: Approved entries are posted to the general ledger
Approval Authority Levels:
  • Standard Entries: Line manager approval required
  • High Value: Senior management approval for entries above threshold
  • Year-end: CFO approval required for EOY processing
  • Adjustments: Enhanced approval for prior period adjustments
